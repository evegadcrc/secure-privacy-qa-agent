# US Privacy Enforcement Actions — Cookie and Tracking Related

## California AG v. Sephora — $1.2M (August 2022)

**The landmark GPC and cookie tracking case.**

- **Violations:**
  - Failed to disclose that it was selling consumer personal information
  - Failed to honor GPC signals as opt-out requests
  - Failed to process opt-out requests via its "Do Not Sell" link
  - Lacked proper service provider agreements
- **Significance:**
  - Established GPC as a legally binding opt-out signal under CCPA
  - Set precedent for all subsequent GPC enforcement
  - Demonstrated CA AG willingness to enforce cookie/tracking violations

**Source:** California AG press release, August 24, 2022. Case: People v. Sephora USA, Inc.

## FTC v. Epic Games (Fortnite) — $520M (December 2022)

- $275M for COPPA violations, $245M for dark patterns
- Used dark patterns to trick players (including children) into purchases
- Manipulative consent flows and deceptive billing practices
- **Relevance:** Dark patterns in consent UIs are enforceable by FTC

## FTC v. BetterHelp — $7.8M (March 2023)

- Shared health data with advertising platforms (Facebook, Snapchat, Criteo, Pinterest) via **tracking pixels/cookies** without adequate consent
- Users promised confidential therapy, but tracking cookies sent data to ad networks
- **Relevance:** Tracking cookies transmitting sensitive data to third-party ad platforms is an enforcement priority

## FTC v. GoodRx — $1.5M (February 2023)

- First enforcement under FTC's Health Breach Notification Rule
- Shared user health data with Facebook, Google, and other advertisers via **tracking pixels and cookies**
- Failed to notify consumers about the sharing
- **Relevance:** Health-related tracking cookies are under heightened scrutiny

## FTC v. Avast — $16.5M (February 2024)

- Collected browsing data through browser extensions and antivirus software
- Sold it to advertising companies via subsidiary Jumpshot
- Data collected via tracking mechanisms sold without adequate consent
- **Relevance:** Even security/privacy products are liable for improper tracking

## California AG v. DoorDash (2023)

- Fined for selling consumer personal information without proper notice and opt-out rights under CCPA
- **Relevance:** CCPA opt-out requirements are actively enforced

## CPPA Enforcement Advisory (2024)

The CPPA issued enforcement advisories reinforcing:
- Businesses **must honor opt-out preference signals** (GPC)
- **Dark patterns** in cookie consent UIs are violations
- **"Do Not Sell or Share" link** is mandatory for businesses sharing data for behavioral advertising
- Noncompliance will result in enforcement action

## Enforcement Trends

1. **GPC non-compliance** is the primary US enforcement vector (Sephora set the precedent)
2. **Tracking pixels/cookies sharing sensitive data** are a high-priority target (BetterHelp, GoodRx)
3. **Dark patterns** are increasingly codified and enforced (Epic Games, CPRA regulations)
4. **Children's data** remains a major enforcement area (COPPA violations carry large fines)
5. **No safe harbor for "we didn't know"** — controllers must verify consent validity
6. **State AG coordination** — multiple states pursuing similar enforcement
