# Planet49 Ruling — CJEU C-673/17

Landmark ruling by the Court of Justice of the European Union establishing the consent standard for cookie placement. The case originated from the German Bundesgerichtshof (Federal Court of Justice), which referred questions to the CJEU.

## Facts

Planet49 GmbH operated a promotional lottery on the internet. Participation required users to:
1. Tick a box to consent to receiving advertising (opt-in, unchecked by default)
2. A second checkbox, **pre-ticked by default**, consented to web tracking via cookies for advertising personalization

The German Federation of Consumer Organisations (vzbv) challenged the pre-ticked cookie consent checkbox.

## Key Holdings

### 1. Pre-ticked Checkboxes Do NOT Constitute Valid Consent (Paragraphs 52-58)

Consent requires an active behavior by the user. A pre-ticked checkbox that the user must uncheck to refuse consent does not constitute an "indication" of the data subject's wishes by "a clear affirmative action."

> The court held that where the user must deselect a pre-ticked checkbox, it cannot be established that consent was actively given.

### 2. Applies Regardless of Whether Cookies Involve Personal Data (Paragraph 71)

Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive protects against any storage/access to the user's terminal equipment, not just personal data processing. This means:
- Even if a cookie does not contain personal data, consent is still required for its placement
- The protection is about the integrity of the user's device, not just data protection

### 3. Required Information Must Include Duration and Third-Party Access (Paragraphs 74-81)

"Clear and comprehensive information" under Article 5(3) ePrivacy Directive must include at minimum:
- The **duration/lifetime** of cookies
- Whether **third parties** may have access to the cookies
- The **purposes** of the processing

### 4. Consent Must Be Specific

Consent given in the context of a promotional lottery cannot be bundled with cookie consent. Each consent purpose must be separately addressable.

## Practical Implications for Audit

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Pre-ticked boxes invalid | Any cookie preferences panel with non-essential categories pre-checked = CRITICAL violation |
| Applies to all cookies | Even "functional" cookies need consent if not strictly necessary |
| Duration must be disclosed | Cookie policy must list lifetime of each cookie |
| Third-party access must be disclosed | Must state which third parties access each cookie |
| Specific consent | Consent for different purposes must be granular |

## Impact on the Industry

Planet49 was the definitive ruling that:
- Ended the "implied consent" era across the EU
- Forced CMPs to implement opt-in models with pre-checked boxes off
- Required cookie policies to include duration and third-party information
- Applied across all 27 EU member states (binding CJEU authority)
