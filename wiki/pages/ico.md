# ICO (Information Commissioner's Office)

The UK's data protection authority, enforcing UK GDPR + PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) post-Brexit.

## Cookie Governance

The UK applies essentially the same consent standard as the EU through **PECR Regulation 6** (the UK's implementation of [[eprivacy-directive|ePrivacy Article 5(3)]]) + **UK GDPR**.

## Key Positions

- **Implied consent is NOT valid** — "by continuing to browse you agree" is rejected
- **Reject must be available** — banners with only "Accept" and "Settings" are non-compliant
- **Equal effort** — rejecting must not require more effort than accepting
- **No legitimate interest for advertising cookies** — consent is the only basis under PECR

## November 2023 Opinion

The ICO specifically called out:
- Cookie banners offering only "Accept" and "Settings" (no reject)
- Consent mechanisms requiring more effort to reject than accept
- "Legitimate interest" claimed for advertising cookies

## Differences from EU Post-Brexit

- UK GDPR mirrors EU GDPR in substance
- PECR is UK domestic law (independent of EU ePrivacy updates)
- ICO enforcement is independent of EU DPA coordination
- UK-EU adequacy decision currently in force
- Any future ePrivacy Regulation would not apply to the UK

## Enforcement Style

Less aggressive than [[cnil]] in fine amounts but active through:
- Enforcement notices and reprimands
- Detailed guidance and audit tools
- Sector sweeps of cookie compliance
- Focus on public sector and large commercial sites
