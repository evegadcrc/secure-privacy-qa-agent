# GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

**EU Regulation 2016/679** — the world's most influential data protection law. Effective May 25, 2018.

## Role in Cookie Consent

GDPR defines the **consent standard** that applies to cookie placement via the [[eprivacy-directive]]. While ePrivacy governs the act of placing cookies, GDPR defines what "valid consent" means.

## Consent Definition (Article 4(11))

Consent must be:
- **Freely given** — no bundling, no [[cookie-walls]], no detriment for refusing
- **Specific** — granular per purpose, not "all or nothing" (see [[cookie-categories]])
- **Informed** — identity of controller, purpose, data types, right to withdraw
- **Unambiguous** — requires a clear affirmative action (no pre-ticked boxes, no silence)

## Key Articles

| Article | Subject | Cookie Relevance |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| 4(11) | Definition of consent | Sets the standard for all cookie consent |
| 6(1)(a) | Consent as legal basis | One of six legal bases; the only one for cookies under ePrivacy |
| 7(1) | Demonstrable consent | Controllers must prove consent was given — see [[prior-consent]] |
| 7(2) | Distinguishable request | Consent request must stand out from other content |
| 7(3) | Right to withdraw | [[Consent withdrawal]] must be as easy as giving consent |
| Recital 32 | Affirmative action | Silence, pre-ticked boxes, inactivity ≠ consent |
| Recital 42 | Burden of proof | Controller must demonstrate valid consent |
| Recital 43 | Freely given | No conditional consent; no power imbalance |

## Relationship to Other Frameworks

- Works with [[eprivacy-directive]] (lex specialis) for cookie-specific rules
- Enforced by national DPAs: [[cnil]], [[ico]], [[edpb]] coordinates
- Interpreted by [[planet49]] ruling (CJEU)
- [[edpb]] Guidelines 05/2020 and 03/2022 provide binding interpretations
- Influenced [[lgpd]], [[coppa]] updates, and many [[global-law-comparison|global privacy laws]]

## Enforcement

GDPR allows fines of up to **4% of annual global turnover** or 20 million EUR, whichever is higher. Cookie consent violations have resulted in fines exceeding 150M EUR (see [[cnil]]).
